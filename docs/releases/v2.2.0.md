# DP Sensor Hub v2.2.0

An operations release: alerts in Telegram, complete long-range charts, power and cost at a glance, and a dashboard redesigned to run on a wall.

## Added

- **Telegram alerts.** Leaks, batteries under 20%, devices that stop reporting, and readings outside limits you set send a Telegram message to everyone signed in to the bot, and another when the condition clears. A condition that flaps back within 30 minutes stays quiet. Turn alerts off in Settings → Alerts & energy.
- **Alert limits.** Set "below" and "above" limits per reading under its history chart. The chart draws the limits, circles every reading outside them, and shows the share of time outside; the dashboard's Needs attention panel lists broken limits.
- **Complete long ranges.** The broker now writes an hourly roll-up (count, average, low, high and watt-hours) alongside each reading. 7- and 30-day charts read these, so they cover the whole period instead of the newest 500 readings, and show each hour's low-to-high band.
- **Chart tools.** Compare up to four lines (other readings of the device, or the same reading on other devices) in at most two units with left and right axes; 1-hour, 6-hour and custom date ranges; drag to zoom; CSV export of every line; a dashed average line.
- **Daily and Pattern views.** Daily shows energy per day in kWh for power (with cost when a tariff is set in Settings) and daily average with low-to-high for other readings. Pattern is a weekday-by-hour heatmap.
- **Power & load overview.** Live total load (W/kW) from smart plugs, total current draw (A) from CT sensors, a bar chart ranking every circuit, and the worst three-phase imbalance from CT301 sensors. Select any bar to open its history chart.
- **Needs attention.** Leaks, low batteries, broken limits and silent devices in one list, or "All clear".
- **Dashboard tools.** Search by name, model, EUI or room; filter by Reporting, Quiet, Silent or Alerts; cards group by room. Set each device's room and report interval in Settings → Devices; a device is quiet after 1.5 intervals and silent after 3 (15 minutes and 2 hours when no interval is set).
- **Wall display.** Full screen, larger type, no controls, and scrolls through the dashboard every 15 seconds. Start it from the dashboard or open the dashboard URL with `#wall`.
- **New look.** A dark operations console by default (slate, status green, frosted panels, Fira Sans and Fira Code, SVG icons), with a light theme on the header toggle. The choice is remembered per browser.
- **Live cues.** A header badge reads **Live · updated Xs ago** and ticks on its own; it turns **Stale** after 15 minutes without an uplink. Device cards show whether each device is reporting, quiet or silent, and a reading flashes briefly when a new uplink changes it. Motion is disabled for people who set reduced motion.

## Upgrade notes

- **Firebase mode: deploy the updated `firestore.rules`.** Devices now accept `limits`, `interval` and `room`; the new `settings/energy` document is writable by its owner; `telemetry/{device}/hourly` is readable by its owner. Local DuckDB mode needs no action.
- Hourly roll-ups start with the first uplink after upgrading. Periods before that chart from raw readings, which are capped at the newest 500 per line; the chart footer says when the cap is reached.
- Energy is integrated from consecutive power readings; gaps longer than 1 hour are not counted.
- Alerts are sent only while the workspace's Telegram bot is running. Alert state is kept in memory, so a restart can repeat a still-active alert once.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts. Servers without internet access fall back to system fonts.
- Upgrade by pulling the new image or code and restarting.
